# Match-then-message format

## How likes and messages work
You cannot message until you **match**. LIKE means pursue; SKIP means
pass. `draft_message` is the **first message after a match**, not
something she sees with the like. Empty `draft_message` on LIKE is
normal and often correct.

## What's on a profile
Photos first (the swipe decision), then a bio, interests/badges, and
sometimes prompts. Many profiles are photos + a short bio only.

## What `target_prompt` (the hook) means
The single best thing to open on: a **bio line**, an **interest**, or a
**photo detail**. Name it clearly. If the bio is empty or generic, hook a
specific photo object, not a category ("your travel photos").

## Screenshot hints
Top-to-bottom as the profile scrolls. In chat, the operator's messages
are usually on the **right**. If that's ambiguous, don't guess.
