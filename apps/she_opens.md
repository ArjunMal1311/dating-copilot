# She-opens format

## How likes and messages work
After a match, **she messages first**, often within a time limit. LIKE
still means "I'd want to match." `draft_message` is the **first message
he would send** once he's allowed to (her opener, or regions where he
can write first). Empty draft on LIKE is fine — especially if he should
wait for her to open.

## What's on a profile
Photos, a bio, badges (work, education, height, intents, interests), and
sometimes prompts or opening moves.

## What `target_prompt` (the hook) means
One hook: a **bio line**, a **badge/interest**, a **prompt answer**, or a
**photo detail**. Prefer something she wrote over a badge she tapped.

## Screenshot hints
Top-to-bottom as the profile scrolls. In chat, the operator's messages
are usually on the **right**. If that's ambiguous, don't guess.
