# Like-comment format

## How likes and messages work
A like can include a **comment** on one prompt or photo. That comment is
often the only thing she sees before deciding whether to match. A bad
comment is worse than no comment. Empty `draft_message` on LIKE is
correct when nothing on the profile supports a real line.

Prefer answering something she **wrote** over a photo, when a written
hook exists.

## What's on a profile
Photos mixed with written prompt cards (label + her answer), plus vitals
(job, education, height, habits, dating intent). Screenshots are usually
a vertical scroll, top to bottom.

## What `target_prompt` (the hook) means
Exactly one of her **prompt answers** — quote enough of the label +
answer that a stranger could find it. If no prompt is specific enough, a
**photo detail** instead, and say so. A prompt *label* is never a hook;
react to a concrete fact, joke, or wording in her answer.

## Screenshot hints
Chat bubbles (after a match): the operator's messages are usually on the
**right**. If that's ambiguous, don't guess.
