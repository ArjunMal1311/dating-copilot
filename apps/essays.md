# Essay format

## How likes and messages work
You can often message without matching, depending on the product. LIKE
means pursue; `draft_message` is the **first message** (or like-note if
that's what the screenshot shows). Empty draft on LIKE is correct when
nothing supports a real opener.

## What's on a profile
Photos, longer essays ("About me" and similar), Q&A, vitals, and
sometimes interests. Essays are usually the richest hooks.

## What `target_prompt` (the hook) means
Exactly one **essay paragraph**, **question answer**, or **photo
detail**. Quote enough that a stranger could find it. Don't open on a
match percentage or a generic "we have a lot in common".

## Screenshot hints
Top-to-bottom as the profile scrolls. In chat, the operator's messages
are usually on the **right**. If that's ambiguous, don't guess.
