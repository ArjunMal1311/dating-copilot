# Research behind the opener rules

Merged from public threads on photos, prompts/openers, and whole-profile
strategy (r/OnlineDating, r/datingoverthirty, r/dating_advice, r/genericapp,
r/Generic, r/FemaleDatingStrategy). Sentiment is women's, paraphrased. App
product details (like-comments, who messages first) live in `apps/*.md` —
this file is the shared quality bar.

The loudest signal: **a low-effort profile is read as a promise of low
effort in a relationship.**

## Photos

Fill every vital the app offers. Blank job / education / height / drinking
/ kids / politics / religion gets the least flattering guess.

A useful photo set, in this order:

1. You alone. Face visible, no sunglasses/hat, real smile, taken by someone else.
2. Full body / waist-up.
3. Doing a real hobby.
4. Social proof — friends, you unmistakable. Never the first photo.
5. Another side of your life.
6. A second clear, current face shot.

Cut: bathroom / mirror / car selfies, every photo a selfie, a woman who
could be an ex, shirtless gym-mirror flex, you not in frame, obvious AI,
stone-face in all six, a drink in every shot, money/status props.

## First messages

One concrete detail + one low-cost question. Under ~25 words. A bad opener
is worse than none — empty draft on LIKE is a valid answer.

Appreciation is being recognised; appraisal is being rated. Never
compliment looks, face, or body. Specificity is the signal: if the line
could go to someone else with no edit, it's wrong.

Don't notice the most obvious headline. Joke-first openers test worse than
oddly specific curiosity. No "hey". No homework questions. No
reassurance-seekers ("lol", "hope that's not too weird").

Never exoticise (ethnicity, "interesting" as a type). Being appreciated as
a category isn't appreciation.

## Icks to delete on sight

"You should NOT date me if…" · bitterness about dating · vague intent ·
self-deprecation · posed shirtless with no activity · money/hustle flex ·
face hidden in most photos · AI-written prompts or filtered photos.

The opener rules in `memory.py` enforce the drafting half of this. App
mechanics (Generic like-comment vs Generic match-then-message) are in
`apps/`.
